How this was calculated

Federal withholding uses the annualized-wage method: annual gross minus the 2026 standard deduction, run through the 2026 marginal tax brackets below, then divided across your pay periods. This mirrors the logic behind the IRS's own percentage-method tables, simplified to the standard deduction only — it does not account for W-4 Steps 2–4 (multiple jobs, dependents, other income/deductions), pre-tax benefits, or state and local tax.

Figures: 2026 federal income tax brackets and standard deduction per IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32.
